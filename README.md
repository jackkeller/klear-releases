# Klear

Downloads and the update feed for Klear, a Mac menu bar app that removes
background noise from your microphone and transcribes calls on your Mac.

- **Download:** the latest DMG is on the [Releases](https://github.com/jackkeller/klear-releases/releases/latest) page.
- **Requires:** macOS 14 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
- **Updates:** Klear checks `appcast.xml` here and updates itself.
